About X-Fence

Built on Foundations
That Don't Move

X-Fence has been putting up fences for over seven years — homes, businesses, you name it. Whether it's a solid closeboard boundary or a sleek slatted design, we build fences that look great and actually last.

Every job starts with a proper look at your site, accurate measurements and an honest chat about what you need. We only use the good stuff — treated timber, concrete posts and heavy-duty gravel boards — because a fence is only as strong as what's holding it up. Think of us as the people who take your boundary personally.

The X-Fence Difference

Why Choose X-Fence?

We don't cut corners — we reinforce them. Every post is straight, every panel is level, and every customer gets exactly what they were promised.

01

Premium-Grade Materials

Treated timber, concrete posts and heavy-duty gravel boards as standard. We'd rather over-build than under-deliver — "flimsy" isn't in our vocabulary.

02

Precision Craftsmanship

Solid foundations, spot-on measurements and real attention to detail. If a fence line isn't dead straight, we're not signing it off. Simple as that.

03

Dependable & Punctual

We arrive when we say we will — a radical concept in the trades, apparently. Clear communication from initial enquiry to final handshake. No ghosting, no excuses.

04

Engineered for British Weather

Wind, rain, frost, the occasional biblical downpour — our installations are built to stand firm through whatever this island throws at them, season after season.

05

Turnkey Service

We handle everything: taking out the old fence, putting in the new one, and clearing up all the mess. You get a clean finish — not a garden that looks like a building site.

06

Transparent Pricing

We look at the job, give you a price, and stick to it. No hidden charges, no pressure, no sneaky extras on the bill. The price we agree is the price you pay.
